# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in the Arcovia community to be welcoming, constructive, and respectful for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give technical feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith engagement.
- Publishing private, sensitive, or confidential information without permission.

## Enforcement

Use GitHub's report-content controls to report harassment, abuse, or other conduct concerns privately. Reports are handled under GitHub's Community Guidelines and may result in a warning, temporary restriction, or removal from project spaces.
